## 📝 **STRING CATEGORIES**

### **1. App & Navigation**
- App name
- Screen titles (Home, Shopping List, Categories)
- Menu items (Upload, Download, Settings, etc.)

### **2. Category Management**
- Category labels
- "Create Category", "Edit Category", "Delete Category"
- Dialog titles and buttons

### **3. Grocery Management**
- "Add to Shopping List", "Mark as Bought"
- "Edit Grocery", "Delete Grocery"
- Field labels (Name, Expiration Date, etc.)

### **4. Store Management**
- "Manage Stores", "Create Store", "Edit Store"
- "Link to Store", "Unlink from Store"
- "Hide Store", "Show Store"

### **5. Group Sharing**
- "Family Group", "Create Group", "Join Group"
- "Group Code", "Copy Code", "Leave Group"
- "Upload to Cloud", "Download from Cloud"

### **6. Dialogs & Confirmations**
- "OK", "Cancel", "Save", "Delete"
- "Are you sure?", "Confirm", "Yes", "No"
- Success/Error messages

### **7. Dates & Times**
- "Expiration Date", "Buy Date"
- Date formats (handled by Android, but labels needed)

## 📄 **NOTES ON TRANSLATIONS**

### **Russian (values-ru/strings.xml):**
- Use Cyrillic script
- Consider formal vs. informal "you" (use informal for app)
- Plurals: one, few, many, other

### **Bulgarian (values-bg/strings.xml):**
- Use Cyrillic script
- Similar to Russian but with differences
- Plurals: one, other

**Tip:** Start with Google Translate, then refine for natural phrasing.

---

## 🔄 **MIGRATION PROCESS**

### **Step 1: Find Hardcoded Strings**
Search for:
- `Text("Hardcoded string")` in Composables
- String literals in UI code

### **Step 2: Add to strings.xml**
```xml
<string name="descriptive_name">Hardcoded string</string>
```

### **Step 3: Replace in Code**
```kotlin
// Before
Text("Hardcoded string")

// After
Text(stringResource(R.string.descriptive_name))
```
